Where the inputs come from

Rainfall: Open-Meteo archive API, model era5_seamless, https://archive-api.open-meteo.com/v1/archive
Alerts: IEM VTEC archive, https://mesonet.agron.iastate.edu/cgi-bin/request/gis/watchwarn.py, states=NC, 2024-09-25T00:00Z to 2024-09-29T00:00Z
Retrieved 2026-09-27T03:49Z. 92 zone-coded alert rows are not mapped to counties; county alert coverage may be incomplete.
Route: OSRM public demo server via stormroute.routing.client, real road geometry; counties from data/sample/nc_counties_sample.geojson (simplified boundaries, about 1 km coarse); stretches from stormroute.routing.sampling and collapse_intervals. Chosen (2026-09-27) to replace the original Asheville-Charlotte pair: that pair's shared origin county (Buncombe) was itself catastrophically hit, which pinned both routes to Severe regardless of path and could not show real route-choice differentiation. This pair's two real OSRM alternates pass through disjoint sets of foothill counties whose real Helene exposure genuinely differs -- one path (via Iredell/Yadkin/Wilkes) stays at High concern, the other (via Gaston/Lincoln/Catawba/Alexander/Wilkes) reaches Severe, a real difference driven by a Wilkes County Flood Advisory that had escalated to a Flood Warning by the time the longer route arrives there.
